# 🌊 JalaRakshak

### An Affordable, Real-Time Early Warning System for Coastal Flooding

**JalaRakshak** is a software prototype developed by **Team BlueSentra** to demonstrate how technology can help improve flood preparedness in vulnerable coastal communities of Odisha, India.

Our interactive simulator demonstrates water-level monitoring, flood-risk detection, predefined safety thresholds, and automatic alert generation. The long-term vision is to integrate the software with physical sensors and microcontrollers to support a real-world flood early-warning network.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Coastal regions of Odisha face flooding risks due to heavy rainfall, cyclones, rising water levels, and storm surges. Delayed warnings and gaps in last-mile communication can leave communities with insufficient time to prepare for potential flooding.

JalaRakshak explores an affordable and accessible approach to demonstrating how early flood warnings could help communities prepare for these hazards.

## 💡 Our Solution

JalaRakshak is an interactive software simulation of a proposed flood early-warning system. It demonstrates how rising water levels can be monitored, classified into risk levels, and used to trigger alerts when predefined safety thresholds are crossed.

The prototype allows users to simulate changing water levels and observe how the system responds to different flood-risk conditions.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Interactive Water-Level Simulation:** Adjust water levels and observe changes in the simulated environment.
- **Multi-Level Risk Detection:** Demonstrates different risk levels using predefined safety thresholds.
- **Automated Alert Simulation:** Shows how crossing a danger threshold can trigger a warning.
- **Siren and Notification Simulation:** Demonstrates the proposed alert mechanism through simulated siren activation and notifications.
- **Disaster Preparedness Focus:** Designed around the needs of flood-prone coastal communities in Odisha.
- **Future Scalability:** Planned integration with physical sensors, microcontrollers, and communication networks.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Simulate:** The user adjusts the water level in the interactive simulator.
2. **Detect:** The software compares the simulated level against predefined safety thresholds.
3. **Generate Alerts:** When a threshold is crossed, the prototype demonstrates the corresponding risk warning and alert response.
4. **Prepare:** The concept illustrates how timely warnings could help residents prepare and move to safer locations.

## 🖥️ Current Development Status

**Current stage: Software Prototype**

The team has developed an interactive software prototype to demonstrate the core concept of JalaRakshak.

- Software simulation of water-level changes and risk thresholds.
- Demonstration of automated warning logic.
- Simulated siren activation and notifications.

**Hardware status:** Physical water-level sensors, microcontrollers, and siren units have not yet been developed or integrated.

## 🚀 Future Scope

- Integrate ultrasonic or other suitable water-level sensors.
- Connect sensors to microcontrollers for real-time monitoring.
- Explore offline communication technologies such as LoRa for transmitting alerts.
- Add location-specific warnings and multiple monitoring nodes.
- Explore solar power and battery backup for remote deployment.
- Test the integrated system in controlled conditions before considering field deployment.

## 🌍 Expected Impact

JalaRakshak aims to contribute to:

- Improved flood preparedness in vulnerable coastal communities.
- Faster communication of local flood-risk information.
- Greater awareness of changing water levels and potential hazards.
- An affordable, scalable approach to community-level early warning.

These are intended benefits. Real-world performance and impact will require hardware development, testing, and field validation.

## 🛠️ Technology and Implementation

The current deliverable is an interactive software prototype. The final software technology stack and hardware components will depend on the implementation and future development stages.
